# Solid-state batteries

## Input

```
/maxi what's the state of solid state batteries for a research agent
```

## Output

````
```
Research question: As of [today's date], how close are solid-state batteries to mass-market use in electric vehicles, and what are the remaining blockers?

Scope:
- Cover: energy density, cycle life, manufacturing cost/scale, and announced production timelines from Toyota, Samsung SDI, QuantumScape, Solid Power, CATL, and any other credible entrant.
- Exclude: semi-solid / hybrid electrolytes unless clearly labeled as such; consumer-electronics cells.

Recency: prioritize sources from the last 12 months. Anything older is background only.

Source bar: peer-reviewed papers, company filings and investor presentations, and reporting from outlets with a track record on battery tech. Treat company press releases as claims, not evidence — flag when a timeline has slipped versus a prior announcement.

Output:
1. One-paragraph verdict with a confidence level.
2. Table: company → claimed timeline → what has actually shipped → independent verification.
3. The three hardest unsolved problems, with the best current evidence on each.
4. What would have to be true for a 2028 mass-market launch.
5. Sources, with a note on which ones disagree and why.
```

Compiled in auto mode for a research agent.

Assumptions:
- "State of" means EV readiness, not the underlying chemistry — say so if you want the materials-science view instead.

Fill in:
- [today's date]
````
